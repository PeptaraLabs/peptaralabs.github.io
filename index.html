Checking any peptide seller in Vietnam

  1. The certificate names a real, independent testing lab.
  2. Its date is recent enough to match current stock.
  3. The lot code can be matched to what you were sent.
  4. HPLC purity comes with its chromatogram.
  5. Mass spec (LC-MS) confirms the molecule's identity.
  6. Refund and reship terms are written down before you pay.

Be wary of chat-only sellers with no certificate, cropped report images, and web addresses one letter away from a brand you know.
